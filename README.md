## Hello World! 
👋 About Me

Hi! I’m Saad Sow, a curious and driven student with a strong passion for STEM. I'm constantly exploring new areas of science and technology—both in school and through hands-on programs outside the classroom.

One of my most impactful experiences was completing the STEM Inventory Lab Program at The Cooper Union’s Albert Nerken School of Engineering. Over 14 weeks, I earned certifications in Mechanics & Electronics, Data Visualization, and Machine Learning & AI. This program gave me my first real exposure to the inner workings of technology—and left me wanting to dive even deeper.

I also interned at Beam Center, where I worked with a team to design a system that delivers food and resources to a fictional island. This project challenged me to think creatively, collaborate effectively, and communicate professionally.

Through the First Tech Fund Fellowship, I took part in workshops covering everything from public speaking and resume writing to financial literacy. I also worked one-on-one with a mentor to plan for life after high school and set clear, achievable goals.

Beyond academics, I volunteer at Room to Grow Daycare, where I help manage inventory and ensure quality supplies are always available. I'm fluent in English, French, and Fulani, which helps me connect with people from different backgrounds and cultures.

Right now, I’m looking for new opportunities in STEM where I can continue learning, connect with others, and grow both personally and professionally.

💡 My Interests

I’m especially fascinated by technology—how it shapes the world we live in, and how it’s constantly evolving. One area that really excites me is cybersecurity. I’m curious about how systems are protected, and how some people manage to bypass even the most advanced security measures. My interest in this field started during the STEM Lab program at Cooper Union and has only grown since then.

I also completed a six-week intensive web development course at The Knowledge House through KKCF, where I learned HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. That experience gave me a strong foundation in front-end development and a deeper appreciation for how websites and apps are built.

Outside of tech, I have a strong interest in sales and communication. I believe sales is more than just selling a product—it’s about building trust, sharing ideas, and influencing decisions. Whether you're presenting yourself in an interview or pitching an idea, sales skills are essential. I’m particularly interested in how sales and technology intersect to create better customer experiences and smarter business strategies.

Exploring the combination of tech + sales is something I’m passionate about, and I believe this blend is where a lot of innovation happens in today’s digital world.
